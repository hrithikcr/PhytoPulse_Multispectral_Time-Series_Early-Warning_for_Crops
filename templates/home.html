Early Crop Disease Risk, Before It Strikes

PhytoPulse anticipates disease risk using vegetation indices (NDVI, GNDVI, EVI), weather (rain, temperature, humidity), and short time-series signals. Enter your readings on the Predict page and get an instant risk band: 🟢 Low / 🟠 Moderate / 🔴 High.

Start Prediction

Problem Statement

Farmers often notice disease only after visible damage, when mitigation is costly. We need an early-warning signal that flags risk before symptoms emerge.

Existing System & Limitations

  • Manual scouting—late detection, human bias.
  • Rule-based thresholds—don’t adapt to context.
  • Fragmented data—indices and weather not fused.

Proposed System & Merits

  • Fuses vegetation + weather + lags for richer signals.
  • Time-aware modeling to anticipate near-term risk.
  • Simple UI: manual input → clear color-coded risk.

How It Works

  1. Collect your field readings (see ranges on the Predict page).
  2. Enter values manually.
  3. Get a risk score and band with guidance to act early.
